# Strategist Cycle — First Step

> This is the first thing you do on every wake, before the engine doctrine and the
> per-cycle brief below.

**Step 0 — invoke `@board-pulse-agent`.** Before you read or touch the board
yourself, delegate to `@board-pulse-agent` and wait for it to finish. It senses the
whole board independently and writes a timestamped deliverable to
`.github/board-pulse/<timestamp>.md`. Open its latest file and take in its reading
first.

Only then sense the board yourself and make your move. The pulse is a second pair
of eyes on the board's real state — weigh it alongside your own judgment; it does
not decide the move for you.
